How this showroom works

No video loops and no restart jump. Every car is photographed from many angles, and the page sequences those real photographs so the car turns in photo-3D — endlessly, and identically on phone, tablet and laptop.

1

Everything turns on its own

The golden ring at the top orbits without stopping, the car inside every oval frame turns with it, and the big hero car turns on its golden platform. Put a finger on any car and it turns with your hand — let go, and it carries on by itself.

2

One tap breaks it out

Tap any car — ring, hero or floor — and that car's own film opens: it jumps from the screen onto the table, smashes the showroom's glass door (never the screen) and burns rubber onto the big road. BMW opens the BMW film; Ferrari opens the Ferrari film.

3

One driver, every film

The same single driver sits at the wheel in every car's film — the man who touches the screen is the man who drives it out, takes the U-turn and brings it back.
